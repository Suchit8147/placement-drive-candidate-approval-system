🔬 Concurrency Conflict Simulation Scenario:

Two administrators concurrently attempt to book the identical physical room (Room-101) for overlapping time intervals. Transaction 1 (Admin A) acquires the row lock first and successfully performs a COMMIT. Transaction 2 (Admin B) discovers that the resource is occupied, aborts execution, and triggers a full ROLLBACK, preserving 100% database integrity.
